## ch2_garden_frozen.png
**Path:** `chapter2/cg/ch2_garden_frozen.png`
**Purpose:** Wide view of the frozen garden when player first enters

```
[STYLE ANCHOR]

Scene: Wide cinematic view of a large abandoned greenhouse / garden
chamber inside an industrial sci-fi facility. The room was once lush
and tropical — hanging vines, tall mechanical plant beds, stone water
basins, ferns on suspended platforms. NOW it is COMPLETELY FROZEN.

Frozen elements throughout:
- Hanging vines turned to frozen icicle cascades
- Mechanical plants caught mid-motion, ice crystals on metal petals
- Water droplets suspended frozen in mid-air
- Frost crawling across walls in fractal patterns
- Pale blue-white frost over everything

A central PAVILION / gazebo stands at the back-center of the scene.
A small PINK figure stands motionless inside it — barely visible,
just a pink presence with a hint of gold (her crown).

Three plant beds visible in the lower-mid area of the frame:
- Plant bed 1 at ~30% from left, ~75% from top
- Plant bed 2 at ~50% from left, ~78% from top
- Plant bed 3 at ~70% from left, ~75% from top

Other features:
- Wall valve system / pipes visible on left side, ~8% left, ~35-65% vertical
- A frozen wooden plaque encased in an ice column at ~18% left, ~60% top
- An ice sculpture (frozen water column) at ~78% left, ~48-62% vertical
- A small mechanical frog on a stone at ~62% left, ~80% top
- A small maintenance vent at ~88% left, ~78% top

Color palette: heavy blues (#7fb8d4), pale frost whites (#d6f2ff),
deep teal shadows. ONE warm pink point in the pavilion (#e84a8c).
Faint warm light leaks from the frog and the plant beds.

Mood: beautiful but eerie. Like Sleeping Beauty's castle but mechanical.
Sad, not horrific.
```
